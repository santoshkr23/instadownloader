FAQ

Is it free?

Yes — no signup, no app, no watermark.

Can I download private posts?

No. Only public posts are supported; private accounts and login-only content can't be fetched.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, files go to the Files app → Downloads. On Android they land in your Downloads folder.

Why does a download fail sometimes?

The post may be private, deleted, region-locked, or the platform may be rate-limiting. Try again in a minute.
